1. Master Prompt #1: "Monday Fuel" (Paste into M365 Copilot with Web Toggle ON)

Make sure the Web toggle is selected at the top of Microsoft Copilot, then paste this exact prompt:

Act as an Ardent Mills Transportation Analyst. Pull this week's Monday Fuel Surcharge rates from the following 3 sources and format them into a clean Consolidated Summary Table with columns: [Carrier / Source | Category | Effective Date | Avg. OHD Price | Rate / Price]: 1. Oceanex (https://www.oceanex.com/forms-references/fuel-surcharge/): - Extract ONLY the Effective Week and the "Full Load TL2 (10,000 – 54,999 lbs)" fuel surcharge rate. 2. CN Rail (https://www.cn.ca/en/customer-centre/prices-tariffs-transit-times/fuel-surcharge): - For Tariff CN 7404 (Weekly Intermodal): Extract Effective Date, Avg. Weekly OHD Price, and the "Intra-Canada" rate ONLY. - For Tariff CN 7405 (Monthly Intermodal): Extract Current & Upcoming Effective Month, Avg. OHD Price, and the "Intra-Canada" rate ONLY. 3. Bison Transport (https://img.bisontransport.com/public/resources/pdf/documents/bison_transport/1033-canada_crossborder_domestic_fuel_surcharge.pdf): - Extract the Effective Date range and the "Intermodal (Within Canada)" fuel surcharge rate ONLY. Do not include US, Cross-Border, LTL, or Carload rates—only the exact Canada/TL2 fields listed above.
Pro Tip if Cloudflare blocks Oceanex or Bison PDF in standalone Copilot: Open the Oceanex, CN, and Bison tabs in Microsoft Edge, click the Copilot icon in the top-right corner of Edge, and paste the prompt there—Edge Copilot reads open browser tabs and PDFs directly without getting blocked by website firewalls!
